The two product images were generated from text, inspected, and included at
their original generated size. No commercial product photograph, brand mark,
reference image or person was supplied to these generation calls. These are
illustrative products, not photographs establishing real material specifications.

Prompt briefs:

- Tote: a minimal unbranded natural cotton canvas tote with two handles,
  photorealistic e-commerce studio lighting, neutral grey background, complete
  product visible, no text or logo, square composition.
- Notebooks: two minimal unbranded notebooks in graphite and pale grey,
  photorealistic e-commerce studio lighting, neutral grey background, complete
  products visible, no text or logo, square composition.

The UI uses project-authored simple SVG stroke icons. The product names,
descriptions, prices and inventory are fictional demo content. No asset from
the excluded commercial brand is needed to run this showcase.
