# Community project boundaries

- This is an independent community distribution. Do not connect tests, fixtures,
  demos or maintenance tasks to a production shop, payment account or customer database.
- Use generated demo assets and clearly fictional data. Keep credentials and
  developer-private configuration out of commits and logs.
- Preserve upstream notices. The scope of Apache-2.0 is documented in NOTICE
  and docs/rights-and-provenance.md.
- Run the documented checks after relevant changes. Report actual results and
  distinguish local mock verification from real WeChat platform acceptance.
- Deployment and external customer messaging require a separate explicit request.
